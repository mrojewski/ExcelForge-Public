ExcelForge

Practical Excel tools, developed and validated step by step.

ExcelForge is an evolving Excel productivity project. We publish product capabilities when they are sufficiently implemented, tested and understood.

A desired capability is not presented as a product capability until it is implemented and sufficiently validated.

Guardian G1

Current status: external pilot within documented boundaries.

Guardian G1 is the first ExcelForge tool prepared for technically aware external pilot use. Its current workflow is delivered as an Excel Add-In with a Quick Access Toolbar launcher and ordinary .xlsx working files.

Pilot packaging is platform-specific: Windows uses one .xlam add-in; macOS uses an .xlam add-in together with its Helper and UI dependency.

Available now

  • Excel Add-In based pilot workflow.
  • Quick Access Toolbar launcher.
  • Validated pilot packaging for Windows and macOS within the tested environments.
  • Validated installation, restart persistence and uninstall/removal paths within the tested environments.
  • Platform-specific deployment guidance.

Known limitations

  • The first-ever macOS permission and security flow on a machine with no prior Guardian history has not yet been fully validated.
  • Managed or corporate device restrictions, including signing and trust policies, are outside the current pilot validation scope.
  • Some workbook structures require further investigation. A known example is an Excel Table totals row being included in the detected data region.
  • Materially changed future technical revisions may require update-path revalidation.

Pilot

The purpose of the pilot is to collect real-use evidence: what works, what fails, where the workflow causes friction and where current assumptions are incomplete.

Feedback is evidence for further investigation and development. It is not a promise that a specific future feature will be implemented.

Download and feedback channels are being prepared.

How we communicate

  • Available capabilities are described as available.
  • Known limitations are stated explicitly.
  • Areas under investigation are not presented as finished features.
  • Aspirations and desired future capabilities are not presented as product facts.
